🔒 Safe & Sound

Our Privacy Promise 🌈

Kids Drawing & Color Fill Game is a fun, safe place to draw and color. Here's our simple, grown-up-friendly promise about how we keep your little artist's privacy protected.

📅 Last updated: June 19, 2026
👋

Who We Are

Kids Drawing & Color Fill Game creates fun, safe, and educational games for children. We are committed to protecting children's privacy and providing a secure experience for kids and families.

🧺

Information We Collect

🚫 No Personal Information Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from children. We do not ask for:

  • ✕ Name
  • ✕ Email address
  • ✕ Phone number
  • ✕ Home address
  • ✕ Photos
  • ✕ Location data
🎨 Children can use our games without providing any personal information — just pick a color and start creating!

📊 Non-Personal Information

We may collect limited non-personal information automatically, such as app usage data (for example, game performance and crash reports).

This information is used only to improve app performance and user experience. It does not identify any individual child.

Our app is designed for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

🚫

Advertisements

Our app does not display third-party advertisements. No pop-ups, no surprises — just creative fun.

🤝

Third-Party Services

This app uses Firebase (by Google) for crash reporting and analytics. Use of the app is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

🛡️

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and other applicable child privacy laws.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
  • If we become aware that personal information has been collected unintentionally, we will delete it immediately.
  • Parents may contact us at any time regarding their child's privacy.
🔄

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made:

  • The updated policy will be posted on this page.
  • We encourage parents to review this policy periodically.
📨

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your child's privacy, please reach out. We are committed to responding promptly and responsibly. 💛
